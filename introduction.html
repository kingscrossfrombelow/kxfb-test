King's Cross from Below: 40 years of community organizing around King's Cross development

Edited by Michael Edwards and Jason Katz

Published by UCL Press, 2026 | Open Access

This book documents over four decades of community activism around the redevelopment of King's Cross in London, told through the voices of 36 contributors who lived, worked, and organized in the area. From the decline of welfare capitalism in the 1970s through the "victory of the new economy" in the 2000s, these accounts capture grassroots resistance, creative alternatives, and the human cost of urban transformation.

The website serves as a living archive with permanent URLs for citation and will be archived by the British Library.
